A102 Facility: Description 
A. The function of the facility is to generate power to operate a data center using Bloom 
Energy Server System fuel cells. This system is a type of solid oxide fuel cell system 
(SOFC) which electrochemically converts natural gas into electricity  using an 
electrochemical reaction.  
B. This facility is located approximately 3.6 miles South of Santa Teresa in New 
Mexico, Doña Ana County.  
C. This permit application is for a N ew Source Review (NSR)  construction permit 
issued pursuant to 20.2.72.200.A(1) NMAC.  The facility will be a microgrid power 
generation facility  that will consist  of a maximum of  2,275 solid oxide fuel cell 
(SOFC) clusters with a total electric generating capacity of 2,462 MW. The electric 
generating capacity of each module is either  260 kW or 325 kW . Fuel cells are 
arranged in cell  stack -> power module - > energy server  -> cluster -> inverter 
group/platform. The description of this modification is for informational purposes 
only and is not enforceable.

D. Method for Determining Compliance with the Startup, Shutdown and Maintenance 
Natural Gas Venting Emission Limit in Section 107.B – (Unit SSM)  
Requirement: The permittee shall comply with the monitoring, recordkeeping, and reporting 
of this condition to demonstrate compliance with the limits from SSM emissions.  
 
(1) The permittee shall calculate the emissions from each event using Equations 1 and 2, the 
maximum volume of natural gas (Vmax) in cubic feet vented per activity, and the VOC 
emission factors for each activity. The permittee shall use the maximum volume of natural 
NSR Permit No. 10883   Page 7 of 19 
 
gas (Vmax) in this condition when determining emissions from SSM events, even if the 
actual volume of gas vented was less.   
 
Note: The maximum volume of natural gas Vmax is the maximum amount of natural gas 
that can be vented from the  specific activity, for example venting during one 
desulfurization startup event based on its physical limitations. Furthermore, the V max of 
natural gas applies per unit of that activity and is not the sum of all natural gas vented 
during the event.  
 
Equation 1:  ([Maximum standard cubic feet (scf)/startup] x [Startup/month]) + 
([Maximum scf/maintenance event] x [Maintenance events/month ]) x [VOC emission 
factor lb/scf] / [2,000 lb/ton] = VOC tons of emissions per month 
 
Where:  
 
Maximum scf/startup = 780,900 Vmax vented from one startup event at one CDS unit.  
 
Maximum scf/ maintenance = 520,600 Vmax vented from one maintenance event at one 
CDS.  
 
Startup/month = The number of total startup events that occur at all CDS units each month. 
 
Maintenance events/month = The number of total maintenance events that occur at all 
CDS units each month. 
 
 
VOC emission factor (lb/scf) = The sum of the emission factors for each VOC constituent 
in the current inlet analysis of the natural gas calculated using Equation 2. 
 
Equation 2:  [Mole Fraction of VOC Constituent] x [Molecular Weight of VOC 
Constituent (lb/lb-mol)] / [379.4 scf/lb-mol]

A110 Facility: Fuel and Fuel Sulfur Requirements

Requirement:

better as certified by the device manufacturer: 
(1) the actual sulfur concentration in the natural gas exiting the Central Desulfurization System

obtaining an extended gas analysis on a representative grab sample, 
(2) the CDS shall be sampled on a rotating basis such that all CDS are sampled during each 
rotation prior to any CDS being re-sampled. 
Recordkeeping:  
The permittee shall keep the following records: 
(1) a current purchase contract  or tariff sheet  listing the type of fuel and its quality 
specifications,  
(2) total measured sulfur content in the natural gas fuel exiting the CDS.

F. Operation and Emission Calculation  
Requirement: 
The permittee shall demonstrate compliance with the allowable emission limits in Table 106.A by 
performing the following monitoring, recordkeeping, and reporting for the facility.  
 
Each Energy Server shall be equipped with a load meter, which shall continuously record the 
electrical output of each Energy Server.  
Monitoring:

Server. 
 
The Permittee shall meet the General Monitoring Requirements in Condition B108. 
Recordkeeping:   
NSR Permit No. 10883   Page 18 of 19 
 
The Permittee shall keep records of the total hourly electrical output of each Energy Server and 
the total for the entire facility. 
 
The Permittee shall use the records of the maximum electrical output per month and monthly hours 
of operation of each Energy Server and the emission rates from stack tests required by Conditions 
A601.D and E to calculate the monthly total ton per year NOx, CO, VOC, PM10, and PM2.5 emissions 
from the entire facility using the following equation:  
E = (EF × H) / 2,000  
Where:  
E = Monthly NOX (or CO, VOC, PM10, and PM2.5) emissions (tons/month)  
EF = NOX (or CO, VOC, PM10, and PM2.5) emission factor ( lb/MW-hr), based on the maximum 
results from the most recent stack tests. 
H = total monthly electrical output (MWhr/mo) 
 
The Permittee shall use the monthly records to calculate the monthly rolling 12-month total ton per 
year NOX, CO, VOC, PM10, and PM2.5 emissions from the entire facility.  
 
Compliance with SO2 emissions will be calculated based on the extended gas analysis results 
obtained annually using the following equations: 
 
SO2 emission rate (lb/MWhr) = F / P / S * MSO2/MS * Y 
 
F = fuel consumption in standard cubic feet per hour 
P = electrical capacity in operation in MW 
S = standard molar volume of natural gas in ft3/lb-mol natural gas, 359 ft3/lb-mol natural gas 
MSO2 = molar mass of sulfur dioxide, 64 lb SO2/lb-mol SO2 
MS = molar mass of sulfur, 32 lb S/lb-mol S 
Y = measured lb-mol S/lb-mol natural gas 
 
SO2 emissions (tons) = SO2 emission rate (lb/MWhr) * H / 2000-lb/ton 
 
H = total monthly electrical output (MWhr/mo) 
 
The Permittee shall maintain records of all required calculations.  
 
The permittee shall maintain records in accordance with the applicable Sections in B109, B110, and 
B111.

The permittee shall report in accordance with the applicable Sections in B109, B110, and B111.
